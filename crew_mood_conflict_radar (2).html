Important Scientific & Ethical Disclaimer

This module is a wellness support and pattern-recognition tool designed to help crews notice early communication friction, repetitive frustration, or social withdrawal. It is NOT a conflict prediction system, nor does it diagnose mental health conditions or assume negative language definitively equals interpersonal conflict.
